Bridging The Gap

Retail Backtests vs. Institutional Reality

Over 90% of retail quantitative strategies fail in live production. Learn the methodological rigor required by top asset managers and systematic hedge funds.

✕ The Flawed Retail Approach
  • ✗ Look-ahead bias: Using 10-K fiscal figures before they are publicly filed.
  • ✗ Survivorship contamination: Testing only currently active surviving tickers.
  • ✗ Zero slippage friction: Assuming infinite liquidity at exact closing prices.
  • ✗ Naive Markowitz inversion: Generating extreme, unstable portfolio weights on noise.
  • ✗ Standard K-Fold leakage: Shuffling temporal bars and predicting the past from the future.
✓ The Institutional Desk Standard
  • ✓ Point-in-Time datasets: Strict publication timestamps avoiding retroactive leaks.
  • ✓ Survivorship-free universes: Full dynamic historical reconstitution.
  • ✓ Microstructure modeling: Kyle's lambda, Almgren-Chriss impact, and ADTV limits.
  • ✓ Covariance regularization: Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage and Hierarchical Risk Parity (HRP).
  • ✓ Purged Walk-Forward CV: Deflated Sharpe Ratio (DSR) correcting for multiple testing.

## 📄 Full Description (Sales Page / Gumroad Store)

### Why Do Most Backtests Fail in Live Production?
Have you ever finished developing a quantitative strategy boasting a 2.5 Sharpe ratio, negligible drawdown, and a pristine equity curve, only to watch its performance completely evaporate in live execution—or get rejected during an institutional hedge fund technical interview?

This breakdown happens for one primary reason: **structural modeling biases**.

Generic online tutorials and retail finance courses systematically ignore the physical frictions of real-world trading: operational latency between signal generation and order execution, financial statement restatement dates, non-linear market impact, and multiple testing bias (p-hacking). In institutional quantitative hedge funds, no strategy receives capital allocation without surviving a relentless data integrity audit.

The **Basic Edition** delivers the comprehensive methodological framework, institutional taxonomy, and governance protocols utilized by professional quantitative desks, structured in a concise, high-density format.

# The Quant Transition Playbook & Institutional Toolkit - Standard Edition
Author: Lucca Simeoni Pavan, Ph.D.
Former Head of Quantitative Strategies & Portfolio Allocation Manager

Contents of this package:
1. All 4 PDFs (Playbook EN/PT + Anti-Bias Checklists EN/PT)
2. 'code/' folder with the 4 Production Quantitative Engines:
   - backtest_multifactor.py & backtest_multifactor_en.py (Value + Momentum vectorized backtester)
   - risk_performance_metrics.py & risk_performance_metrics_en.py (CVaR 95%, Sortino, Max Drawdown suite)
   - factor_orthogonalization_fwl.py & factor_orthogonalization_fwl_en.py (Frisch-Waugh-Lovell alpha filter)
   - ledoit_wolf_covariance.py & ledoit_wolf_covariance_en.py (Ledoit-Wolf covariance shrinkage)
   - requirements.txt (Dependencies for single-command installation)
   - README_TOOLKIT.md & README_TOOLKIT_EN.md

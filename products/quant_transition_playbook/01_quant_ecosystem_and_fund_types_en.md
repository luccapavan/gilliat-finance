# Module 1: The Quant Ecosystem & Fund Business Models

## 1. The Taxonomy of Quantitative Strategies

Unlike traditional discretionary funds where allocation decisions stem from committee debates and qualitative narratives, the quantitative industry operates strictly guided by empirical data, econometric validation, and algorithmic execution. The primary investment paradigms include:

### 1.1 Factor Investing / Smart Beta
* **Core Thesis:** Long-term excess risk-adjusted returns originate from systematic exposures to compensated risk anomalies (Momentum, Value, Quality, Low Volatility, and Size).
* **Trading Frequency:** Low to Medium (monthly or quarterly rebalancing).
* **Core Skillset:** Cross-sectional panel econometrics, accounting multiple normalization, matrix algebra, and constrained portfolio optimization.

### 1.2 Statistical Arbitrage (StatArb) & Pairs Trading
* **Core Thesis:** Economically linked asset pairs share long-run price equilibria; temporary structural deviations create high-probability mean-reversion trading opportunities.
* **Trading Frequency:** Medium to High (intraday to multi-day horizons).
* **Core Skillset:** Cointegration testing (Engle-Granger, Johansen), state-space modeling (Kalman Filters), and market microstructure execution.

### 1.3 Trend-Following / CTAs (Commodity Trading Advisors)
* **Core Thesis:** Macro and global asset classes (Interest Rate Futures, FX, Commodities, Equity Indices) exhibit persistent multi-month price trends driven by institutional capital flows.
* **Trading Frequency:** Medium to Long-Term.
* **Core Skillset:** Time-series momentum modeling, dynamic volatility scaling, and systematic drawdown control.

## 2. Quantitative Desk Roles & Career Paths

1. **Quantitative Researcher (Quant Researcher / QR):**
   * Formulates economic hypotheses, tests novel alternative data feeds, models factor alphas, and rigorously proves that edge survives out-of-sample testing.
   * *Common Background:* Ph.D./M.Sc. in Economics, Finance, Statistics, Physics, Mathematics, or Engineering.
2. **Quantitative Developer (Quant Developer / QD):**
   * Engineers mission-critical infrastructure: ultra-fast data ingestion pipelines, low-latency execution engines, order routing APIs, and automated rebalancing schedulers.
   * *Common Background:* Computer Science, Software Engineering. Core Stack: Python, C++, Rust, SQL, Docker.
3. **Quantitative Risk Manager (Risk Officer):**
   * Independently monitors portfolio stress bounds: historical scenario stress-testing, Value-at-Risk / Expected Shortfall modeling, liquidity horizons, and sector risk limits.
